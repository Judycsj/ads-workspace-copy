## Instructions/使用说明

Please send the report on a monthly basis. It should be sent via email, cc'ed level by level to Product Line Head (Andy) and Team Head. For each update, please update the progress monthly in the same document, using Google Docs heading format (Heading) to mark each month for easy tracking. You may refer to the grey instructional text under each report section as a guide when writing your content.

## Email Template/邮件模板

- **E-mail Title**: `New Comer Report - {Your name} - {Your team} - {Sending date}`
- **To**: `{your leader}`, `{your mentor}`, `{+2 leader}`, `{+3 leader}`
- **Cc**: `{your HRBP}`, miao.huang@shopee.com, andy.zhanggx@shopee.com, `{Team Leader email}`
