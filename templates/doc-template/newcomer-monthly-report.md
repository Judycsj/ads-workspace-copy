## Instructions/使用说明

**(CN)**
请月度发送 Report。需通过 E-mail 发送，逐级抄送至 Product Line Head (Andy) and Team Head。每次更新请在同一个文档中按月更新进展，使用 Google Docs 的标题格式（Heading）来标明每个月，方便追踪。你可以参考每个汇报章节下方的灰色说明文字，作为撰写内容的指导。

**(EN)**
Please send the report on a monthly basis via email to leaders (copy Product Line Head (Andy) and Team Leader). Please update your monthly progress in the same document each time. Use Google Docs heading titles (Heading) to label each month's update for easy tracking. You may refer to the grey instructional text under each report section as a guide when writing your content.
