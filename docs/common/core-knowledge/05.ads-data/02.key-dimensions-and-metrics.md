## 5.2 Key Dimensions & Metrics

This section defines the core dimensions and metrics used in ads data analysis, establishing a unified data language.

### 5.2.1 Key Dimensions

#### Item

The promoted product entity. A product's Category, Brand, and Attributes are important analysis dimensions.

#### User

- **User (Buyer)**: A purchaser on the Shopee platform, including sellers acting as buyers. User journey: Install → Register → Active.
- **Seller**: Registered through Seller Center for listing items, purchasing ads, and managing accounts.

#### Shop

Shop and Seller are distinct concepts: 1 seller maps to N shops (one-to-many relationship).

#### Ads

Ads placed on the Shopee App/Web, designed to increase product exposure and improve purchase probability.

#### Campaign

An ad group sharing budget, objectives, and strategy. Types include Product Ads, Shop Ads, Display Ads, etc.

#### Pricing Type

The bidding mode of an ad, such as CPC (Cost Per Click), CPM (Cost Per Mille), OCPM (Optimized CPM), CPS (Cost Per Sale).

#### Entrance

The specific frontend area where ads are displayed (e.g., search results page entrance=1, Daily Discover entrance=3). Note: currently set by the backend in json_data due to frontend tracking complexity.

#### Placement

Can refer to: 1) the ad display area; 2) the ad type; 3) a specific ad type within a specific area. Derived from entrance via mapping.

#### Additional Dimensions

| Dimension | Description | Source |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| `product_type` / `sub_product_type` | Ad product line classification, derived from `pricing_type + placement` | `dim_product_type_mapping` |
| `main_traffic_type` / `traffic_type` | Traffic type classification, split by entry_point | `dim_entry_point_mapping_v2` |
| `seller_tier` | Top seller tier | `dim_shop_info` |
| `seller_cluster` | Clustering-based seller comparison group | `dim_advertiser` |

> For full dimension definitions, see [DPM Common Dimension Dictionary](../../../team/20.paid-ads-dpm/ads_knowledge_base/shared/common-dimension-dictionary_cn.md)

### 5.2.2 Atomic Metrics

| Metric | Field Name | Definition |
|--------|------------|------------|
| Impressions | `impression_cnt` | Number of ad impressions |
| Clicks | `click_cnt` | Number of ad clicks |
| Add-to-Cart | `atc_cnt` | Number of add-to-cart actions |
| Ad Spend | `expenditure_amt` | Advertiser's ad expenditure (pre-tax, excluding Display/Expiry) |
| Direct Orders | `direct_order_cnt` | Number of directly attributed orders with ad clicks within 7 days |
| Broad Orders | `broad_order_cnt` | Number of broadly attributed orders with shop ad clicks within 7 days |
| Direct GMV | `direct_order_gmv_amt` | GMV of directly attributed orders (local currency, seller_gmv basis) |
| Broad GMV | `broad_order_gmv_amt` | GMV of broadly attributed orders (local currency, seller_gmv basis) |

### 5.2.3 Composite Metrics

| Metric | Abbreviation | Formula | Description |
|--------|--------------|---------|-------------|
| Click-Through Rate | CTR | Clicks / Impressions × 100% | Probability of an ad being clicked |
| Conversion Rate | CR | Conversions / Impressions × 100% | Probability of an ad generating a conversion |
| Cost-Income Ratio | CIR | Ad Costs / GMV × 100% | Ratio of ad spend to GMV |
| Cost Per Click | CPC | Ad Costs / Clicks | Average cost per click |
| Cost Per Mille | CPM | 1000 × Ad Costs / Impressions | Cost per thousand impressions |
| Return on Ad Spend | ROAS | Broad Ads GMV / Ad Costs | GMV generated per unit of ad spend |
| Ad Load | AL | Ads Impressions / Total Impressions × 100% | Proportion of ad impressions in total impressions |
| Take Rate | TR (Take Rate) | Ad Costs / Platform GMV × 100% | Proportion of ad revenue in platform GMV |
| Advertiser Value | ADVV | Broad Ads GMV / Target ROI | Equivalent value of the advertiser at target ROI |
| PC2 | PC2 | PC1 - GTF | Platform profitability metric after transaction fees (see §5.2.5) |
| Valid Overall Take Rate | VOTR | (MP Revenue - Smart Voucher) / Platform GMV | R&D-controllable net monetization efficiency |
| Marginal Cost Rate | MCR | ΔPC2 loss / ΔGMV Uplift | Incremental PC2 cost per unit of GMV uplift from voucher issuance |
| CPIGMV | CPIGMV | Smart Voucher Cost / GMV Uplift | Voucher cost efficiency (coupon cost per unit GMV uplift) |

### 5.2.4 GMV Concepts

Multiple GMV concepts exist in the ads system; the specific basis must be clarified when used:

| GMV Concept | Definition | Use Cases |
|-------------|------------|-----------|
| **seller_gmv** | Seller perspective, merchandise value. Formula: `merchandise_subtotal - seller rebate/coins + shopee item rebate` | **Recommended external basis**: ads attribution GMV (broad_gmv, ads_order_gmv, paid_order_gmv) |
| **buyer_checkout_gmv** | Buyer checkout perspective, including shipping, insurance, buyer fees, minus various rebates | **Internal basis**: Take Rate denominator (platform_gmv), algorithm evaluation, traffic efficiency |
| **platform_gmv_995_v2** | buyer_checkout_gmv plus P99.5 order-level outlier truncation (winsorized) | Experiment evaluation, platform value analysis |

> Note: The legacy field name `platform_gmv` maps to the `buyer_checkout_gmv` concept; it is not a separate concept.

> For GMV reconciliation details, see [DPM GMV Reconciliation Guide](../../../team/20.paid-ads-dpm/ads_knowledge_base/shared/guides/gmv-reconciliation-guide_cn.md)
> For full metric definitions, see [DPM Common Metric Dictionary](../../../team/20.paid-ads-dpm/ads_knowledge_base/shared/common-metric-dictionary_cn.md)

### 5.2.5 PC2 & Profitability Metrics

#### PC2 Definition and Formula

PC2 is the platform's core profitability metric after deducting transaction fees:

```text
PC2 = PC1 - Gross Transaction Fee (GTF)
PC1 = MP Revenue + 3PL Margin - PRM excl. 3PL Margin - RSF/RTS
```

Key components of MP Revenue (~17.3% of GMV):

| Component | ~% of GMV | Description |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Mandatory Commissions | ~8.2% | MP + Mall + CB mandatory commissions |
| Optional Commissions | ~3.6% | MP + Mall + CB optional commissions |
| Handling Fees | ~1.8% | Seller + Buyer handling fees |
| Net Paid Ads | ~3.4% | Ads revenue net of costs |
| Other | ~0.3% | Marketing, digital products |

Key cost components (PRM excl. 3PL Margin, ~16.4% of GMV):

| Component | ~% of GMV | Controllable by |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Logistic PRM | ~7.9% | Local operations |
| Discount Voucher PRM | ~8.0% | Smart Voucher (R&D) + Local Voucher (Local ops) |
| Coins PRM + Other | ~0.5% | Mixed |

#### R&D vs Local Responsibility Boundary

- **R&D team**: Controls MP Revenue optimization (GMV-driven commissions + Net Paid Ads) and Smart Voucher cost.
- **Local team**: Controls Logistic PRM, Local Voucher, and other operational expenditures.
- **Valid Overall Take Rate** = `(MP Revenue - Smart Voucher) / Platform GMV` isolates R&D-controllable items for clean attribution.

#### Voucher Economics: Three-Stage Model

Voucher issuance follows a three-stage economic model with respect to platform profitability:

| Stage | Budget Scale | PC2 Impact | GMV Impact | Decision |
|-------|-------------|------------|------------|----------|
| Stage 1 | Low | PC2 grows | GMV grows | Increase investment (win-win) |
| Stage 2 | Medium (past PC2 tipping point) | PC2 loss | GMV still grows | Evaluate marginal cost rate |
| Stage 3 | High (past GMV tipping point) | PC2 loss worsens | GMV declines | Do not enter (net loss) |

In Stage 2, the decision depends on the **Marginal Cost Rate** = `ΔPC2 loss / ΔGMV Uplift`, which represents the PC2 cost of each additional unit of GMV. Local teams set the acceptable upper bound for this rate.
