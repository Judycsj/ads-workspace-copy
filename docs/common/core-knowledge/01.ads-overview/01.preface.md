## 1.1 前言 / Preface

<!-- last_translated: 2026-06-05T09:27:07.063441+00:00 -->

As Shopee's advertising business and technology continue to iterate and upgrade, and with the adjustment and consolidation of related business and personnel divisions, the current Paid Ads documentation has become scattered and lacks structure, making systematic learning increasingly costly. This document is positioned as an **overview of advertising business and technology**, providing a detailed walkthrough of ads engineering, algorithmic technology, and business topics. It aims to help algorithm engineers, software engineers, and business colleagues quickly understand the core engineering architecture, model strategies, business algorithms, and related processes of the advertising system.

Throughout the learning process, readers are encouraged to pay attention not only to the algorithms and engineering technology itself, but also to the underlying business logic and design philosophy behind them, trying to look beyond the local context to understand more systemic issues. Gradually establish the right business mindset and thinking habits, so that in future optimization work, based on deeper algorithmic understanding and business insight, you can improve and innovate the system from a more holistic perspective.
