## 1.6 Shopee Paid Ads 业务介绍 / Shopee Paid Ads Business Overview

### 1.6.1 流量入口和形态 / Traffic Entry Points and Formats

#### 1.6.1.1 商品广告 / Product Ads

<!-- last_translated: 2026-06-05T10:26:11.444371+00:00 -->

Product Ads are Shopee's most prominent ad format, appearing in scenarios such as search results pages and recommendation pages. Product Ads are Shopee's highest-spending performance ad type, covering both Search and Recommendation (RCMD) scenarios. They support multiple bidding modes including Manual CPC, eCPC, Target ROI, and GMV MAX (see [§1.6.2](#162-广告投放产品和链路概览-ad-campaign-products-and-pipeline-overview)), using GSP second-price auction with click-based billing. Suitable for all sellers looking to boost product exposure and sales — from first-time SME advertisers to established brand merchants — making it the go-to ad type for new advertisers.

![Product Ads traffic entry points](./img/Xnip2026-03-20_16-34-18.jpg)

#### 1.6.1.2 店铺广告 / Shop Ads

<!-- last_translated: 2026-06-05T10:26:09.118721+00:00 -->

Shop Ads appear in the form of shop cards in scenarios such as search results pages. Shop Ads are shop-level promotions that drive users to the shop page to browse more products, with the recommendation subject being a "Shop × Item" combination. Suitable for sellers looking to increase overall shop traffic and brand awareness, especially multi-category shops and sellers with frequent new product launches.

![Shop Ads traffic entry points](./img/Xnip2026-03-20_16-42-37.jpg)

#### 1.6.1.3 品牌广告 / Brand Ads

<!-- last_translated: 2026-06-05T10:26:09.994565+00:00 -->

Brand Ads appear in visually prominent formats such as banners at the top of search results and on the homepage. Brand Ads are display ads with reserved slots (see [§1.2.3.1](02.online-advertising-fundamentals.md#1231-classification-by-delivery-mechanism)), not dynamically blended with organic content. They use GFP first-price auction with click-based billing, delivering strong visual impact. Suitable for brand advertisers with larger budgets and brand-building objectives, with typical use cases including campaign-period brand exposure, new product launches, and brand event promotions.

![Brand Ads traffic entry points](./img/Xnip2026-03-20_16-42-49.jpg)

#### 1.6.1.4 直播广告 / Live Ads

<!-- last_translated: 2026-06-05T10:26:15.244211+00:00 -->

Live Ads appear in the form of live room promotion cards in scenarios such as recommendation pages. Live Ads promote live streaming rooms, leveraging real-time host interaction to drive user conversions. The recommendation subject is "Stream room × Item", with impression-based billing. Suitable for sellers with live streaming capabilities, particularly effective during mega sales events, new product launches, and regular shop live sessions that require real-time engagement.

![Live Ads traffic entry points](./img/Xnip2026-03-20_16-43-01.jpg)

#### 1.6.1.5 视频广告 / Video Ads

<!-- last_translated: 2026-06-05T10:26:09.711652+00:00 -->

Video Ads appear in short video format in scenarios such as recommendation pages and video feeds. Video Ads use short videos as the content medium (see [§1.2.3.2](02.online-advertising-fundamentals.md#1232-classification-by-content-type)), offering rich information and strong user attention. The recommendation subject is "Video × Item", with impression-based billing using GSP second-price auction. Suitable for sellers with video content production capabilities, driving product discovery and conversion through content seeding, especially effective for visually appealing categories such as fashion, beauty, and food.

![Video Ads traffic entry points](./img/Xnip2026-03-20_16-43-18.jpg)

### 1.6.2 广告投放产品和链路概览 / Ad Campaign Products and Pipeline Overview

<!-- last_translated: 2026-06-05T10:26:55.441450+00:00 -->

|      | Product Ads                                           | Brand & Shop Ads            | Video Ads                                                              | Live Ads                                                      |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Auction Products | Target ROI2, Simple ROI2, Manual CPC& eCPC, Shop GMV Max | Auto Mode, Manual CPC& eCPC | Max View, Max GMV                                                      | Max GMV 2.0, Target 2.0, Max GMV 1.0, Target 1.0, Max View    |
| Billing Form | GSP (second-price auction, multi-slot) | GFP (first-price auction, single slot) | GSP (second-price auction, multi-slot) | First-Price (with rankcap & bidcap upper bound constraints, multi-slot auction) |
| Billing Point | click (gradually migrating to impression) | click | impression | impression |
| Delivery Scenarios | Search: SRP; RCMD: DD / YMAL / Game / Cart_Unify | SRP / Game / Display | RCMD: DD / YMAL / Minifeed / ForYou / VideoPage; Homepage; Search; PDP | Discover / For You / Autolanding / Video Tab / Homepage / PDP |
| Recommendation Subject | Item | Shop x Item | Video x Item | Stream room x Item |

> Note: The traffic entry points above cover explicit placements only — those advertised to advertisers on the ad platform. In practice, ad supply with excess budget will be delivered through implicit placements in other traffic entry points (e.g., Product Ads will be implicitly placed in Shop/Live/Video traffic entry points).
